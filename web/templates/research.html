Screens — buy low, and where the experts disagree

Screens, not rankings — each one is a question generator. Promoting any of these to a ranking input needs a measurement this repo has not made, and the last two fitted models here both came back null. Read against the {{ season }} season, taken from config.SEASON rather than a literal.

Points over expected — the buy-low screen

Actual production minus what his opportunity was worth. A large negative is usually the better buy: the volume was there and the points were not, and volume persists better than efficiency at every position. A large positive is efficiency that has to be given back — at RB it repeats at only r=0.28.

How fresh is the panel?

The panel is not evenly fresh, and a plain mean hides it. updated_at is per analyst per player — on 2026-08-10 the median projection was 7 days old for Jason, 64 for Andy and 90 for Mike. Coverage is uneven too, which is why n_analysts rides on every consensus row rather than averaging over a different set of people for every player.

The gate every feature has to clear. Year-over-year correlation, season-forward, highest first. Opportunity runs 0.52–0.65 at RB; the best quality metric tops out at 0.402. This is the measurement behind the Big Board's choice to display opportunity and to treat the order inside a block as a tiebreak rather than a result.
